# Privacy Policy

Smash collects nothing. That's the point of it.

## What we collect

Nothing. There's no account, no analytics, no ad SDK, no crash reporting that sends anything to us, and no tracking. We never get your name, your sessions, your scores or anything else you type into the app.

## Where your data lives

- By default, everything you log stays on your device in SwiftData.
- Turn on iCloud sync and it goes into **your** iCloud account through CloudKit. Apple's privacy terms cover that storage. Smash has no server of its own and can't read your iCloud contents.
- Your profile — name, skill level, avatar and activity goal — is mirrored to iCloud key-value storage when you're signed in, so it follows you to your other devices. Without an iCloud account it stays on the device. This is separate from the Storage toggle, which controls your match and session history.
- Backups are files you export yourself. They sit on your device until you share them. If you put a password on a backup, it's encrypted on your device with a key made from that password. We never see the password or the file.
- Your profile picture is an icon that ships with the app, with a colour you choose. Smash doesn't ask for photo-library access.

## Reviews and links

**Write an app review** opens Apple's StoreKit sheet. Anything you write goes to Apple, not to us. GitHub opens an external site with its own privacy policy.

## Children

Smash isn't aimed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes in the repository and the app. There's no data on our side to migrate, since we don't hold any.
